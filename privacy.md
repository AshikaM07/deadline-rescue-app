# Privacy

Deadline Rescue keeps your data on your phone.

- **No account.** You don't sign in.
- **No servers.** Your deadlines, subjects, steps, name and settings are stored only on your device. The app doesn't send them anywhere.
- **No analytics, ads or tracking.**
- **Notifications** are scheduled on your phone. No server is involved.
- **Backups** are files you create yourself. They go only where you choose to send them. Any app you share them with has its own privacy policy.

Uninstalling the app deletes its data, apart from any backups you exported.

The optional account planned for a later version would change this. This page will be updated before that happens.
